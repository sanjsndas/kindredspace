Kindred Space Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026

Kindred Space respects your privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use, store and share personal information when you contact us, use our services or visit our website. It applies to our interior design business in England.

  1. Who we are

Kindred Space is the organisation responsible for your personal information (the data controller). If you have questions about this policy or wish to exercise your privacy rights, please contact us using the contact details or contact form provided on our website or in your communications with us.

  1. Personal information we may collect

Depending on how you interact with us, we may collect:

• Identity and contact details, such as your name, email address, telephone number and postal address. • Project information, such as property details, design preferences, measurements, photographs, plans, budget and project correspondence. • Transaction information, such as services requested, invoices and payment records. Payment card details are generally handled by our payment provider rather than stored by us. • Information you provide when making an enquiry, booking a consultation, completing a form or communicating with us. • Technical and usage information, such as your IP address, browser type and information about how you use our website, where collected through cookies or similar technologies.

Please do not send us sensitive personal information unless it is necessary for your project and you have agreed to do so.

  1. How and why we use your information

We may use personal information to:

• Respond to enquiries and provide consultations, interior design services and related project support. • Prepare proposals, manage projects, communicate with you and fulfil our agreements. • Process payments, maintain business and accounting records, and meet legal obligations. • Improve our services and website, and protect against fraud or misuse. • Send marketing communications where permitted by law. You can opt out at any time using the unsubscribe option or by contacting us.

We rely on one or more of the following lawful bases under UK data protection law: taking steps at your request before entering into a contract; performing a contract with you; complying with a legal obligation; our legitimate interests in operating and improving our business, provided those interests do not override your rights; and your consent, where consent is required.

  1. Sharing your information

We may share relevant information with service providers who help us run our business, such as website hosting, IT, payment, accounting and project-support providers. We may also share information where necessary with professional advisers, insurers, authorities or other parties when required by law or to establish, exercise or defend legal claims.

We require service providers to protect personal information and use it only for authorised purposes. We do not sell your personal information.

  1. International transfers

If a service provider processes your information outside the United Kingdom, we will take steps required by applicable data protection law to ensure appropriate safeguards are in place.

  1. How long we keep your information

We keep personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, including to provide services, maintain appropriate records and meet legal, accounting or reporting requirements. Retention periods depend on the type of information and the circumstances. When information is no longer needed, we will securely delete it or anonymise it where appropriate.

  1. Cookies and similar technologies

Our website may use cookies or similar technologies to function, understand website usage or support other features. Where required by law, we will ask for your consent before using non-essential cookies. You can also manage cookies through your browser settings. Disabling some cookies may affect website functionality.

  1. Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you may have the right to request access to your personal information, correction of inaccurate information, deletion, restriction of processing or transfer of certain information. You may also object to processing based on legitimate interests and withdraw consent where processing is based on consent. You can opt out of direct marketing at any time.

To exercise a right, contact us using the details described in section 1. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO), the UK's data protection regulator, at ico.org.uk. We would appreciate the opportunity to address your concerns first.

  1. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

  1. Children's information

Our services and website are not intended for children, and we do not knowingly collect children's personal information unless it is necessary for a project and provided by an appropriate parent or guardian.

  1. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will be made available through our usual communication channels or on our website, with the updated date shown above.
